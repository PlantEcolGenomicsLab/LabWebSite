[Biodiversity Research Center, Academia Sinica](https://biodiv.sinica.edu.tw/) in Taipei. We study
how plants evolve and adapt, combining field sampling, genome sequencing and AI (see our

## Wet-lab manager / technician {#lab-manager}

*Full-time · Master level · renewable annual Contract · Start from January 2027 · Open until filled*

We are looking for a hands-on, organized person to set up and run our wet lab. You will be the
lab's molecular biology anchor: turning plant samples from the field, botanical gardens and
herbaria into high-quality DNA for genome sequencing.

### What you will do

- **DNA extraction, routinely,** from freshly collected plant tissue, silica-dried specimens and
  herbarium samples.
- **Develop and optimize protocols,** for example high-molecular-weight DNA for long-read
  sequencing and degraded DNA from old herbarium specimens.
- **Quality control and sequencing,** assessing DNA quantity and integrity, and coordinating
  long-read (e.g. PacBio HiFi) and short-read (Illumina) sequencing with the institute's core
  facility.
- **Sample management,** keeping track of samples, metadata and storage.
- **Running the lab,** managing chemicals, consumables and orders, lab space and equipment, and
  safety and waste procedures.
- **Training others,** helping students and interns learn lab protocols.

### What we are looking for

- A master degree in life science, plant biology, genetics or molecular biology.
  New graduates are welcome.
- Hands-on experience with DNA extraction and basic molecular biology techniques, for example
  from your thesis research.
- Careful, organized work habits, and enjoyment of troubleshooting protocols.
- Good communication in Mandarin; working English for reading protocols and talking with
  collaborators.

**Nice to have:** experience with plant tissue (polysaccharide- or polyphenol-rich samples),
high molecular weight DNA preparation, herbarium or other degraded samples, sequencing library preparation, or
lab management; interest in joining field trips.

### What we offer

- **A founding role** in a new lab, with real ownership over how the wet lab is built and run.
- **Training** in genomic techniques and exposure to field biology, bioinformatics and AI.
- **A collaborative environment** at one of the leading biodiversity institutes in Taiwan, with
  strong core facilities.
- **Salary** from NT$44,968 per month, following Academia Sinica's pay scale for master level
  research assistants; relevant prior experience can be counted toward a higher starting step.
  Labor and health insurance and year-end bonus as per institute rules.

## PhD students {#phd}

We welcome PhD students interested in plant evolution, genomics, biodiversity or machine learning.
Students join through the
**[Taiwan International Graduate Program (TIGP) in Biodiversity](https://tigp-biodiv.biodiv.tw/)**,
an English-taught PhD program run by Academia Sinica with partner universities. It welcomes both
Taiwanese and international applicants.

Before applying, please email {{< email subject="PhD inquiry" >}} with your CV and a few
sentences on which of our [research themes](../research/) interests you, so we can talk about
possible projects.

## Undergraduate interns {#interns}

Undergraduates can join us through the
**[BRCAS Summer Internship program](https://biodiv.sinica.edu.tw/zh_Summer_Internship)**, a
chance to spend a summer doing hands-on research: field and lab work, genomics or computational
projects. When you apply, list our lab as your preferred host, and feel free to email
{{< email subject="Summer internship" >}} to ask about project ideas.

We do not currently have funding for a dedicated postdoc position, but we would be glad to develop

If our research excites you, email {{< email subject="Postdoc fellowship" >}} with your CV and a
